I’m Hao Zhang (张昊), a mechanical engineer and founder of the Mangzhong (芒种) robotics team. I earned my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering and my M.Sc. in Energy and Power Engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, completing my master’s in March 2026.

I started volunteering in the Tengger Desert in 2020. Dig a hole in dry sand and it begins to fill before you can lower a seedling. That observation shaped our first planter: a hollow drill opens only when it reaches planting depth, then lets the sand settle back around the roots. I lead mechanism design, vehicle integration and field trials. We are now adapting the planter for mangrove restoration in Zhanjiang and developing a strawberry harvesting robot with a grower in Nanjing.

<p lang="zh-CN">我是张昊，上海交通大学机械工程本科、能源动力硕士，芒种团队创始人。从腾格里沙漠的志愿植树开始，我和团队一直在做一件事：把现场最费人力的那道工序交给合适的机器。我负责机构设计、整车联调和沙地试验，目前也在推进湛江红树苗移栽和南京草莓采摘机器人的研发。</p>
