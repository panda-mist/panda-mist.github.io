AgiBot · June–September 2023 / 智元机器人 · 2023年6月至9月

I contributed to the thumb mechanism of the A1 humanoid’s 12-degree-of-freedom SkillHand and produced engineering drawings for 43 parts. I also helped test the torque, speed and vibration of three joint motors.

在智元A1人形机器人项目里，我参与的是灵巧手SkillHand的拇指机构设计。这只手共有12个自由度。我为其中43个零件绘制了工程图，也和同事一起测试三款关节电机的扭矩、转速与振动。
