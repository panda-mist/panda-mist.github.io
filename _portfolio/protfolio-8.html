Project lead · January–June 2021 / 项目负责人 · 2021年1月至6月

I designed the mechanical structure and built a complete experimental setup to test materials and mechanisms for a temperature-responsive hydrogel. We developed the hydrogel to use factory waste heat to circulate water in a forward-osmosis system for treating industrial wastewater. The team won a national third prize in the Energy Conservation and Emissions Reduction Competition.

我负责机械结构设计，搭建了整套实验装置，用于测试温敏水凝胶材料与工作机构。团队研制的水凝胶利用工厂余热驱动水循环，通过正渗透处理工业废水。本项目获全国节能减排竞赛三等奖。
