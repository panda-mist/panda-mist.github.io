Summer camp mentor / 夏令营指导教师

I mentored two summer camp students who developed a tai chi coaching and assessment system using human pose recognition and machine learning. They won first prize at the 4th Yangtze River Delta Youth Artificial Intelligence Olympic Challenge.

我指导两名夏令营学员利用人体姿态识别与机器学习开发太极拳指导及评估系统。学员获第四届长三角青少年人工智能奥林匹克挑战赛一等奖。
