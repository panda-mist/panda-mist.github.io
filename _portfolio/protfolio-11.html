Project lead · February–July 2021 / 项目负责人 · 2021年2月至7月

An Arduino Uno controls the gripper as it lifts each meal bag and hangs it at the base of the door handle. In prototype tests against a conventional robotic arm, equipment and computing costs fell by 90%, handling speed rose by 30% and accuracy improved by 10%.

我用Arduino Uno控制筷式夹取机构，提起餐袋并挂到门把手根部。样机与常规机械臂的对照测试中，设备与计算模组成本降低90%，操作速度提升30%，准确率提升10%。
