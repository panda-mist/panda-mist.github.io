collection: portfolio ---

Project lead · February–July 2021

An Arduino Uno controls a gripper that takes a bagged meal from a bin and hangs it at the base of a door handle. In prototype comparisons with a conventional arm, equipment and computing costs fell by 90%, handling speed rose by 30% and accuracy improved by 10%.
