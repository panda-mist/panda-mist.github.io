The paper gate folds flat as a greeting card. Opening the card raises the gate, turning the flat cut-and-fold pattern into a three-dimensional structure.

我把校门做进了一张贺卡。合上时，门楼顺着折痕叠平，藏在两页纸之间。打开贺卡，整座门就立了起来。
