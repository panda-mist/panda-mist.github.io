I cut and folded a single card so the paper gate lies flat when the card is closed and rises into a three-dimensional model when it opens.

我把校门做进了一张贺卡。合上时，门楼顺着折痕叠平，藏在两页纸之间。打开贺卡，整座门就立了起来。
