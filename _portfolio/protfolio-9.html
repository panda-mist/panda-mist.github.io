Engineering intern, LENORET Shanghai Nuotong · July–September 2021 / 上海诺通（LENORET）工程实习生 · 2021年7月至9月

During my internship, I independently designed a 100 kW cascade heat pump for hot and cold water supply. I performed the thermal calculations, selected components, consulted manufacturers, created 3D models and engineering drawings, and simulated the complete system. I received an Outstanding Professional Internship award for this work.

在上海诺通实习时，我独立设计了一套100 kW复叠式热泵，为系统供应冷热水。先做热力计算，再和厂家核对部件参数，选定型号后完成三维模型、工程图和整机仿真。我凭这项工作获评优秀专业实习。
