--- title: "Strawberry Harvesting Robot · 草莓采摘机器人" excerpt: "A six-camera vision system and two parallel robotic arms are being designed to reach ripe strawberries behind unripe fruit and transfer them gently into trays." collection: portfolio mz_order: 3 ---

The CCTV production team introduced us to Golden Manor in Lishui, Nanjing, whose strawberry base covers over 5,200 mu (about 347 hectares). In the greenhouse, a ripe strawberry can hang behind an unripe one. Picking it means finding an approach that clears the fruit in front and supporting it all the way into the tray.

From the row to the tray

Our design uses six RGB cameras to view the growing rows from several angles. Two parallel robotic arms work on opposite sides of the aisle. Each arm approaches a berry from the side and supports it from below. A soft tray receives the fruit to reduce bruising during transfer.

经央视节目组牵线，我们走进南京溧水金色庄园。团队正在研发六相机视觉系统、双侧并联机械臂和柔性托盘，重点解决两个难题：从未熟果后方采到成熟果，以及采下后怎样轻放入托。
