Project lead, mechanical design · February–June 2020 / 项目负责人、机械设计 · 2020年2月至6月

I designed a folding, telescoping fixture for people who cook with one hand. A wrench mechanism and friction lock let users adjust the grip continuously for objects of different sizes. This holds a bottle steady while they turn the cap or stabilizes a vegetable for slicing. The unit also provides storage and a phone stand.

我为单手下厨的使用者设计了可伸缩、可折叠的夹具。通过扳手机构与摩擦锁紧，夹持尺寸可连续调节，既可固定瓶身以便拧开瓶盖，也可固定蔬菜以便切配。装置还提供收纳和手机支架功能。
