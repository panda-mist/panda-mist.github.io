Founder and project lead, September 2023–present · 创始人、项目负责人，2023年9月至今

In loose sand, a planting hole can collapse before a worker can place a seedling. Our hollow rotary drill enters the sand with its tip closed and opens at planting depth. As the drill retracts, sand falls back over the roots. The hinge occupies 5% of the bore, leaving the center open for the seedling.

在干沙里挖坑种树，苗还没放下去，沙子就可能把坑填上。我们把下苗的通道做进钻头里。钻尖合拢入沙，到预定深度再张开，让苗沿中空钻腔落下。提钻后，回落的沙子正好盖住根。开合钻尖的铰链只占钻腔空间的5%，苗才有足够的地方通过。

From hand feeding to automatic planting · 让车自己送苗

We first tested handheld equipment, then fitted the planting mechanism to a tracked vehicle. The automatic feeder carries four rolls of 200 seedlings, enough for 800 seedlings per load. I led the mechanical and electrical integration and field trials in Minqin, Gansu, and Alxa, Inner Mongolia. We adjusted planting depth, seedling release and drill withdrawal for each site and seedling type.

手持栽植器在沙地里试过后，我们把它装上了履带车。但只要还靠人工递苗，就总得有人跟着车走。于是我们做了苗卷，先把苗排好，每卷200株，一车装四卷。我负责整车机电联调，也带队去甘肃民勤和内蒙古阿拉善试车。沙况和苗型不同，栽多深、何时放苗、怎样提钻，都要到地里调。
