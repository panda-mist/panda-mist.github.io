创始人、项目负责人，2023年9月至今 Founder and project lead, September 2023–present

让苗赶在沙子前面 Planting in loose sand

在干沙里挖坑种树，苗还没放下去，坑就塌了。我们把下苗的通道做进钻头里。钻尖合拢入沙，到栽植深度才张开，苗顺着中空钻腔落下。等钻头提起来，回落的沙子正好盖住根。为了让苗顺利通过，开合钻尖的铰链只占钻腔空间的5%。

Our hollow rotary drill enters loose sand with its tip closed. At planting depth it opens to release the seedling. As the drill withdraws, the sand settles around the roots. The hinge takes up just 5% of the bore, leaving the center clear.

让车自己送苗 From hand feeding to seedling reels

从手持钻头做到整车，起初还是得有人跟在车旁，一株株递苗。于是我们把苗预先排成卷，每卷200株，一车装四卷。我负责整车机电联调，也带队去甘肃民勤和内蒙古阿拉善试车。遇到不同的沙、不同的苗，栽多深、何时放苗、怎样提钻，都得重新调。

Our first vehicle still needed someone walking alongside to feed each seedling by hand. We developed a feeder that carries four reels of 200 seedlings each. I led vehicle integration and field trials in Minqin, Gansu, and Alxa, Inner Mongolia. At each site we adjusted the depth, release timing and drill withdrawal for the local sand and seedlings.

让拖拉机边走边旋耕 Continuous rotary tillage for tractors

我们还试过另一种做法，让机器往前走，旋耕机构一路作业，中途不用停车。下图是这条路线的试验机。下一步，我们计划把旋耕机构做成后挂模块，由拖拉机等大型设备牵引作业。

In a separate line of work, we tested a rotary-tillage mechanism that works continuously as the machine advances. The prototype is pictured below. We plan to adapt this mechanism into a rear-mounted implement for tractors and other large machines.
