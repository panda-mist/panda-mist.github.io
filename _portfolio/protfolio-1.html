Founder and project lead · September 2023–present

In loose sand, a planting hole can collapse before a worker has time to place the seedling. We designed a hollow rotary drill that enters the sand with its tip closed. At depth, the tip opens to release the seedling. When the tool retracts, sand settles back over the roots. The hinge occupies just 5% of the bore, leaving room for the seedling to pass through.

From hand feeding to an automatic planter

We first tested handheld equipment, then fitted the planting mechanism to a tracked vehicle. Our feeder carries four rolls of 200 seedlings each. I led the mechanical and electrical integration and trials in Minqin, Gansu, and Alxa, Inner Mongolia.

中空钻头解决了“穴还没栽就塌了”的问题，自动苗卷则减少了跟车递苗的人力。每卷预装200株，四卷共800株。我们在甘肃民勤和内蒙古阿拉善开展现场试验，再按沙地和苗型调整栽深、下苗时序和提钻动作。
