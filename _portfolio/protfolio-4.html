Project lead · December 2018–April 2019 / 项目负责人 · 2018年12月至2019年4月

Inspired by Mohist mechanical designs, I led the development of a cube that reuses the same components for two forms of locomotion. A stepper motor drives the transformation from the compact block into walking and rolling configurations. Our team won first prize in the 2019 National Mind Olympics.

受墨家机关启发，我带队做了这个能变形的方块。步进电机带动内部机构，让同一组零件变换位置。方块能迈步向前走，变形后也能靠滚动前进。我们凭这件作品获得2019年全国头脑奥林匹克竞赛一等奖。
