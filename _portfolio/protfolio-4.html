Project lead · December 2018–April 2019 / 项目负责人 · 2018年12月至2019年4月

Inspired by Mohist mechanical designs, I led the development of a cube that reuses the same components for two forms of locomotion. A stepper motor drives the transformation from the compact block into walking and rolling configurations. The project won first prize in the 2019 National Mind Olympics.

受墨家机关启发，我带队设计了一个方块模块，用同一组零件实现两种运动形式。步进电机驱动方块变形，使其可以步行或滚动。项目获2019年全国头脑奥林匹克竞赛一等奖。
