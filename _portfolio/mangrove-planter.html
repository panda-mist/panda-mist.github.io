On the tidal flats of Zhanjiang, workers carrying mangrove seedlings can sink deep into the mud. At the invitation of the Guangdong Zhanjiang Mangrove National Nature Reserve, we visited the planting sites to study how workers reach each planting point during the low-tide window.

Design in progress

We are adapting the feeder and hollow planting drill from our production desert planter. A broad, hull-shaped chassis spreads the machine’s weight across the mud. Our next tests focus on how far the chassis sinks, how reliably the feeder handles wet root balls, and whether seedlings remain upright as the drill retracts.

受保护区管理局邀请，我们到湛江考察了低潮时的种植作业。项目沿用治沙机的供苗与栽植模组，重新设计船式底盘。当前重点是让机器在软泥中通过，并在拔钻时把带土红树苗留在正确的深度和位置。
