Field research and prototype development, 2026–present · 实地调研与样机研发，2026年至今

On the tidal flats of Zhanjiang, workers carrying mangrove seedlings can sink deep into the mud. At the invitation of the Guangdong Zhanjiang Mangrove National Nature Reserve, we visited the planting sites to study how workers reach each planting point during low tide.

在湛江滩涂，工人抱苗行走时常深陷软泥。受广东湛江红树林国家级自然保护区管理局邀请，我们实地考察了低潮时段的种植作业，了解工人如何携苗到达每个种植点。

Design and testing · 设计与试验

We are adapting the seedling feeder and hollow planting drill from our production desert planter. A broad, hull-shaped chassis spreads the load across soft mud. We plan to measure chassis sinkage, test whether the feeder can handle wet root balls, and check seedling depth and stability after the drill withdraws.

我们沿用量产治沙机的供苗与栽植模组，重新设计船式宽底盘，使载荷分布在更大的滩面上。下一步将测量底盘沉陷量，测试湿重根团能否顺畅下苗，并检查提钻后的栽深与苗木稳固程度。
