Project lead · February–September 2023 / 项目负责人 · 2023年2月至9月

I designed the flipper mechanism and integrated a communications buoy, a buoyancy bladder and a removable watertight electronics housing into the amphibious robot. Eight waterproof servos drive its front-crawl-inspired flippers.

我设计了鳍肢机构，将通信浮标、浮力调节气囊和可拆卸的水密电子舱集成到两栖机器人上，以八个防水舵机驱动鳍肢，模拟自由泳划水动作。
