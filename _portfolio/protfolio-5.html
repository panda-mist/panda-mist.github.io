December 2023–February 2024 / 2023年12月至2024年2月

The wheel takes its cue from the way a cat extends and retracts its claws. A camshaft deploys the claws when a smooth wheel loses traction. They dig into loose sand or mud and push it backward to help the vehicle move forward or free itself.

这一轮式机构借鉴了猫爪伸出与收回的动作。普通轮面抓地不足时，凸轮轴使轮爪伸出，插入并向后推开软沙或泥土，帮助车辆前进或自行脱困。
