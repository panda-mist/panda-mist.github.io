December 2023–February 2024 / 2023年12月至2024年2月

I modeled the wheel on the way a cat extends and retracts its claws. I fitted a camshaft in the hub to deploy the claws when the wheel loses traction. They dig into loose sand or mud and push it backward to help the vehicle move forward or free itself.

猫走路时可以收起爪子，需要抓牢时再伸出来。我借来这个动作，在轮毂里装了一根凸轮轴，让轮爪能伸也能收。遇到软沙或泥地，轮爪伸出后随车轮向后推土，让打滑的车辆有机会重新向前走。
