Project lead · June–December 2020 / 项目负责人 · 2020年6月至12月

I designed a T-slot latch to keep the gripper facing the same way as the retrieval mechanism turns. The mechanism extends in three directions to three times its stowed length, bringing shoes within reach of a standing user.

鞋在柜子里的位置不同。取鞋机构需要转向，夹爪却要稳稳保持原来的朝向。我用T形槽卡扣锁住夹爪，伸缩机构转到位后，可以朝三个方向分别伸出，最长达到收拢时的三倍，把鞋送到老人身前。

Our team won a national first prize in the 9th National Undergraduate Mechanical Innovation Design Competition.

我们凭这款鞋柜获得第九届全国大学生机械创新设计大赛全国一等奖。
