--- title: Assistive Shoe Cabinet for Older Adults excerpt: A telescoping mechanism helps users retrieve and put on shoes while standing.
collection: portfolio ---

Project lead · June–December 2020

A T-slot latch keeps the gripper oriented as it turns. The mechanism extends in three directions to three times its stowed length. The team received a national first prize in the 9th Undergraduate Mechanical Innovation Design Competition.

Award announcement from the School of Mechanical Engineering
